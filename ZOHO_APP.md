## Installation Process

### Step 1: Install from Zoho Marketplace
1. Visit the Zoho Marketplace listing for Multichannel Sales & Inventory Manager
2. Click **Install**
3. Choose your Zoho data center (if prompted)
4. Authorize the required permissions:
   - **ZohoInventory.FullAccess.all**: Sync products, inventory, and orders
   - **ZohoBooks.FullAccess.all**: Sync sales and financial data (optional)

### Step 2: Organization Selection
If you have multiple Zoho organizations:
1. Select which organization to connect
2. Each organization requires a separate installation
3. You can install multiple times for different organizations

### Step 3: Complete Onboarding
1. Set your password for the Multichannel Manager dashboard
2. Configure business preferences (timezone, currency, notifications)
3. Review the onboarding tutorial (optional but recommended)

### Step 4: Connect Sales Channels
1. Navigate to **Channels** in the dashboard
2. Click **Add Channel** for each marketplace you want to sell on
3. Authorize each channel connection:
   - **eBay**: OAuth with your eBay seller account
   - **Amazon**: Enter MWS credentials or use SP-API
   - **Shopify**: OAuth with your Shopify store
   - **Etsy**: OAuth with your Etsy shop
   - **Walmart**: Enter Walmart Seller API credentials
   - **Square**: OAuth with your Square account (POS & online)

### Step 5: Initial Inventory Sync
1. The app will automatically import your Zoho Inventory items
2. Review imported products in the **Products** tab
3. Choose which products to publish to which channels
4. Run your first bulk publish operation

### Total Setup Time
- **Basic Setup**: 15 minutes
- **Full Multi-Channel Setup**: 30-60 minutes
- **First Product Publish**: 5-10 minutes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need a Zoho Inventory or Zoho Books subscription?**
A: Yes, you need an active Zoho Inventory or Zoho Books account. The app integrates with your existing Zoho account.

**Q: Can I use this with multiple Zoho organizations?**
A: Yes! Each Zoho organization requires a separate app installation, but you can manage multiple organizations.

**Q: Which Zoho data centers are supported?**
A: All Zoho data centers: .com (US), .eu (Europe), .in (India), .au (Australia), .jp (Japan), .ca (Canada)

**Q: Does this replace Zoho Inventory?**
A: No, it enhances Zoho Inventory by adding multichannel capabilities. Zoho remains your central inventory system.

### Channel Questions

**Q: Which sales channels are supported?**
A: eBay, Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, Walmart Marketplace, and Square (both online and POS).

**Q: Can I add more channels later?**
A: Yes, you can connect and disconnect channels at any time.

**Q: Do I need seller accounts on each marketplace?**
A: Yes, you need active seller accounts on any marketplace you want to sell on.

**Q: Can I set different prices for different channels?**

**Q: How fast does inventory sync?**
A: Real-time! Stock updates sync within 5 seconds across all connected channels.

**Q: Can I reserve inventory for specific channels?**
A: Yes, you can allocate specific quantities to specific channels.

**Q: Does it support variants (size, color, etc.)?**
A: Yes, full support for product variants across all channels.

A: Orders appear in the unified dashboard and optionally sync to Zoho Books for accounting.

**Q: Can I fulfill orders from the app?**

**Q: Do marketplace fees get tracked?**
